SupplyAI

An inventory system I built for Aisun Solar's Manila and Cebu branches.

The company had stock in two branches, and it was hard to check what we had before promising an order to a client. So I built a web app that tracks each item per branch. Wires are counted in meters, but staff can also enter them in rolls since that is how they are sold.

I am now adding quotes and approvals to it. I decided that discounted quotes should still go to a manager, since a discount is a judgment call and not just a rule. I also removed an AI receipt scanner I added because it was not accurate enough.
